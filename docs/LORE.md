# Lore — South Park: Living Town

## The lorebook byte-identity rule

`assets/lorebook.json` ships byte-identical in every build unless a
lore change is deliberately audited. Its md5 is pinned per build;
currently **acff1c04e84dfd583dbc5b2e20ed50b5**. Any byte change —
even whitespace — breaks the pin on purpose: the lorebook is the
game's canon text, and silent drift is how canon rots.

## Re-audit checklist (run in full for any lorebook change)

1. md5 of `assets/lorebook.json` before and after; the new md5 is
   recorded in `docs/CHANGELOG.md` with the reason for the change.
2. LoreBook entry count: **3,322** entries (any change explained).
3. Alias resolution: **98 cast + 27 location** aliases resolve
   through `LoreAliases` with no dangling target.
4. Regenerate only via `tools/gen_lorebook.py` from the audited
   source, then re-verify the generated bytes end to end.
5. The spoiler scan still passes over the new text.
6. Any byte change requires a full re-audit note in
   `docs/CHANGELOG.md` — what changed, why, and the new md5.
